About Dancescene

Professional training.
Friendly atmosphere.

Dancescene has been part of Northampton's dance community since 2007. Led by Miss Sophie, the school welcomes complete beginners and experienced dancers alike, with classes designed to develop technique, confidence and a genuine love of performing.

From first steps in baby ballet through to optional annual ISTD grading examinations, adult tap and major stage performances, dancers are encouraged to grow at their own pace while feeling part of a supportive dance family.

FAQs

Before your first class

Can my child try a class before joining?

Yes. Contact Miss Sophie through the enquiry form and she can advise on the most suitable class and trial availability.

Do beginners need previous dance experience?

No. Dancescene welcomes beginners as well as dancers with previous experience.

Does Dancescene offer exams?

Yes. ISTD is Dancescene’s exam board, with grading examinations held annually for dancers working towards them.

Are there adult classes?

Yes. Adult tap is currently available, with final timetable details to be confirmed.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held at Northampton School, Thorpeville, Moulton, Northampton, Northamptonshire, NN3 7TR.
